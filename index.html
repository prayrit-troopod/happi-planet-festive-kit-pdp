How to Use

1. Kitchen Cleaner - Spray the foam onto the desired surface. Let it rest for a few seconds. For stubborn stains, allow up to 2 minutes. Wipe the surface with a clean, wet cloth.

2. Tap Cleaner & Limescale Remover Spray the foam onto the affected area. Let it rest for 60 seconds. - Scrub gently and rinse thoroughly with water. Wipe the surface dry with a clean cloth.

3. Reusable Kitchen Towel Pull out one towel from the pack. Wipe spills, grease or everyday messes. Wash the towel with soap and water after use. Squeeze gently, allow it to dry and reuse.

4. Washing Machine Cleaner & Descaler Remove one tablet from its wrapper and place it directly inside the empty washing-machine drum. Run a complete wash cycle without adding clothes. Wipe away any remaining residue with a dry cloth.

5. Copper, Brass & Bronze Cleaner Apply the cleaner evenly using a wet cloth or sponge. Gently scrub with a non-metal scrubber if required. Rinse thoroughly with water and wipe dry.

6. Magic Eraser Soak the Magic Eraser in water. Gently squeeze out the excess water. Wipe the stained area gently until the mark disappears.
